Answer key

  1. 20
    Let n = the number. Translate: n = 0.25 × 80 Multiply: n = 20 Answer: 20
    Hints
    1. Remember that 'is' means equals and 'of' means multiply.
    2. Translate this to: n = 0.25 × 80
    3. Multiply 0.25 by 80.
  2. 30%
    Let p = the percent. Translate: 18 = p × 60 Divide by 60: p = 18 ÷ 60 = 0.3 Convert to percent: 0.3 = 30% Answer: 30%
    Hints
    1. Let p = the percent you are looking for.
    2. Translate: 18 = p × 60
    3. Divide both sides by 60, then convert to a percent.
  3. 120
    Let b = the number. Translate: 42 = 0.35 × b Divide by 0.35: b = 42 ÷ 0.35 = 120 Answer: 120
    Hints
    1. Let b = the number you are looking for.
    2. Translate: 42 = 0.35 × b
    3. Divide both sides by 0.35.
  4. $14.4
    Let t = sales tax Translate: t = 0.06 × 240 Multiply: t = 14.4 Answer: $14.4
    Hints
    1. You need to find 6% of the phone price.
    2. Translate: tax = 0.06 × 240
    3. Multiply to find the tax amount.
  5. $9
    Let d = discount Translate: d = 0.20 × 45 Multiply: d = 9 Answer: $9
    Hints
    1. You need to find the discount, which is 20% of the original price.
    2. Translate: discount = 0.20 × 45
    3. Multiply to find how much you save.
  6. 50 grams
    Let a = total recommended daily amount Translate: 8 = 0.16 × a Divide by 0.16: a = 8 ÷ 0.16 = 50 Answer: 50 grams
    Hints
    1. Let a = total recommended daily amount.
    2. Translate: 8 = 0.16 × a
    3. Divide both sides by 0.16.
  7. 20%
    Find the increase: 14.40 - 12 = 2.40 Let p = percent increase Translate: 2.40 = p × 12 Divide by 12: p = 2.40 ÷ 12 = 0.20 Convert to percent: 0.20 = 20% Answer: 20%
    Hints
    1. First, find the amount of increase: new amount - original amount.
    2. The increase is what percent of the original amount?
    3. Translate and solve: increase = p × 12, then convert to percent.
  8. 25%
    Find the decrease: 80 - 60 = 20 Let p = percent decrease Translate: 20 = p × 80 Divide by 80: p = 20 ÷ 80 = 0.25 Convert to percent: 0.25 = 25% Answer: 25%
    Hints
    1. First, find the amount of decrease: original amount - new amount.
    2. The decrease is what percent of the original amount?
    3. Translate and solve: decrease = p × 80, then convert to percent.
  9. 20%
    Find the increase: 3.00 - 2.50 = 0.50 Let p = percent increase Translate: 0.50 = p × 2.50 Divide by 2.50: p = 0.50 ÷ 2.50 = 0.20 Convert to percent: 0.20 = 20% Answer: 20%
    Hints
    1. Find the amount of increase: 3.00 - 2.50.
    2. The increase is what percent of the original fare of $2.50?
    3. Set up the equation: increase = p × 2.50, then solve and convert to percent.
