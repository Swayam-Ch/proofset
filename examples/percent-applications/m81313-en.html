Answer key

  1. 18
    Translate: n = 0.30 × 60 Multiply: n = 18 The answer is 18.
    Hints
    1. Change 30% to a decimal by dividing by 100.
    2. Multiply the decimal by 60.
    3. 30% means 0.30, so multiply 0.30 × 60.
  2. 25%
    Translate: 18 = p × 72 Divide both sides by 72: p = 18 ÷ 72 = 0.25 Convert to percent: 0.25 = 25% The answer is 25%.
    Hints
    1. Set up the equation: 18 = p × 72
    2. Divide both sides by 72 to find the decimal form of p.
    3. Convert the decimal to a percent by multiplying by 100.
  3. 75
    Translate: 15 = 0.20 × b Divide both sides by 0.20: b = 15 ÷ 0.20 = 75 The answer is 75.
    Hints
    1. Change 20% to a decimal: 0.20
    2. Set up the equation: 15 = 0.20 × b
    3. Divide both sides by 0.20 to find b.
  4. $5.40
    Translate: tax = 0.12 × 45 Multiply: tax = 5.40 The tax was $5.40.
    Hints
    1. Change 12% to decimal form.
    2. Multiply the decimal by the bill amount.
    3. 0.12 × 45 = 5.40
  5. 50 grams
    Translate: 24 = 0.48 × b Divide both sides by 0.48: b = 24 ÷ 0.48 = 50 The total recommended daily amount is 50 grams.
    Hints
    1. Set up the equation: 24 = 0.48 × b
    2. Divide both sides by 0.48
    3. 24 ÷ 0.48 = 50
  6. 37.5%
    Translate: p × 320 = 120 Divide both sides by 320: p = 120 ÷ 320 = 0.375 Convert to percent: 0.375 = 37.5% The answer is 37.5%.
    Hints
    1. Set up the equation: p × 320 = 120
    2. Divide both sides by 320.
    3. Convert the decimal to a percent.
  7. $10
    Translate: savings = 0.25 × 40 Multiply: savings = 10 The customer saves $10.
    Hints
    1. Change 25% to decimal form: 0.25
    2. Multiply the discount percent by the original price.
    3. 0.25 × 40 = 10
  8. 30%
    Find the increase: 260 - 200 = 60 toys Find the percent increase: p = 60 ÷ 200 = 0.30 = 30% The percent increase is 30%.
    Hints
    1. First find the increase: new amount - original amount
    2. Then divide the increase by the original amount.
    3. Convert the decimal to a percent.
  9. 10%
    Find the decrease: 50,000 - 45,000 = 5,000 Find the percent decrease: p = 5,000 ÷ 50,000 = 0.10 = 10.0% The percent decrease is 10.0%.
    Hints
    1. First find the decrease: original amount - new amount
    2. Then divide the decrease by the original amount.
    3. Convert the decimal to a percent and round to the nearest tenth.
  10. 10%
    Find the increase: 16,500 - 15,000 = 1,500 people Find the percent increase: p = 1,500 ÷ 15,000 = 0.10 = 10.0% The percent increase is 10.0%.
    Hints
    1. Find the increase by subtracting: new amount - original amount
    2. Divide the increase by the original amount (15,000).
    3. Convert the decimal to a percent and round to the nearest tenth.
